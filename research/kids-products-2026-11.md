## What Andromeda means for product choice

- Meta's retrieval system reads the creative itself and groups near-identical ads as one. Meta upgraded it on 2026-10-28 (AdScale).
- Winning setup: broad targeting, 4 to 6 genuinely different creatives per ad set (AdScale, observational), catalog ads.
- So pick products that are easy to show in a short video and that suit several different buyer reasons (new parent, grandparent gift, working mom, learning, travel).

## Filters used

1. Real demand in India (Amazon.in bestseller rank and rating count).
2. Easy to demo on video in 3 seconds.
3. Can be sold as a kit or bundle at Rs 799 or more (Amazon sells single items at Rs 120 to 600).
4. Light, hard to break, low return risk.
5. Avoided: diapers, wipes, formula, skincare (big brands, cosmetics licence), feeding bottles (BIS licence), cheap single plush (Amazon is cheaper).
